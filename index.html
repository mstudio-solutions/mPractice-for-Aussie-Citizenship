Introduction

mPractice for Aussie Citizenship helps you get ready for the Australian Citizenship Test. Practise as many times as you like with questions based on the testable sections of Our Common Bond. Large buttons and clear text make it easy to use for everyone.

Features

  • 0 practice questions across Parts 1–4
  • Full Mock test – 20 questions, 5 Values questions, 75% + all Values correct to pass
  • Practice by Part, Values only, Key questions or Random
  • Timer per question, or untimed
  • Fixed answer layout, with optional shuffle
  • Review your mistakes with explanations
  • No sign-up, no ads, no cookies

Terms & Conditions

Privacy Policy

mPractice for Aussie Citizenship by mStudio · Last updated 8 October 2026

The short version

mPractice does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, no ads and no cookies. We count visits anonymously.The iPhone app does not collect any data at all.

What we collect

No personal information. All questions are built into the app. Your answers and scores are never sent to us or to anyone else. We only see anonymous visit counts (see below).

Your practice history

Recent scores are kept only in your browser while the page is open. They are cleared when you close the tab. Nothing is saved on a server.

Your recent scores, your settings and the questions you have already done are saved only on your device, so you can carry on next time. They are never sent to us. You can remove them with the Clear button under Recent Sessions, or by deleting the app.

Cookies, analytics and ads

The iPhone app does not use cookies, analytics, tracking or advertising.

mPractice does not use cookies or advertising. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics ↗ to count visits anonymously. It does not use cookies and does not collect personal information. We only see totals, such as page views, country, browser and whether you use a phone or a computer. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy ↗.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical data (such as your IP address) for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement ↗. The iPhone app is downloaded from the App Store, and Apple's own privacy policy applies to the App Store.

Links to other sites

The app links to the Department of Home Affairs website. Their own privacy policy applies when you visit it.
