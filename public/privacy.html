Privacy Policy

mPractice for Aussie Citizenship (website and iOS app) by mStudio · Last updated 8 October 2026

The short version

mPractice does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, no ads and no cookies. The iOS app does not collect any data at all.

What we collect

No personal information. All questions are built into the app and the website. Your answers and scores are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Your practice history

iOS app: your recent scores, your settings and the questions you have already done are saved only on your device, so you can carry on next time. They are never sent to us. You can remove them with the Clear button under Recent Sessions, or by deleting the app.

Website: recent scores are kept only in your browser while the page is open. They are cleared when you close the tab.

Cookies, analytics and ads

iOS app: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Website: no cookies and no advertising. We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits anonymously. It does not use cookies and does not collect personal information. We only see totals, such as page views, country, browser and whether you use a phone or a computer. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical data (such as your IP address) for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The iOS app is downloaded from the App Store, and Apple's own privacy policy applies to the App Store.

Children

mPractice does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Links to other sites

The app and the website link to the Department of Home Affairs website. Their own privacy policy applies when you visit it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
