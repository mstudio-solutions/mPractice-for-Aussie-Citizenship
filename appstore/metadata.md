## Description (4000)

Get ready for the Australian citizenship test with mPractice.

mPractice has 111 practice questions based on the testable sections of the official book Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond. Many questions have a short explanation, and you can review every mistake at the end.

FULL MOCK TEST
- 20 questions with 3 answers each, like the real test
- 5 Australian values questions in every mock test
- To pass, you need 75% and all 5 values questions correct (the same rule as the real test)
- Answers and explanations at the end, like the real test

PRACTISE YOUR WAY
- 4 parts of Our Common Bond: Australia and its people, democratic beliefs, government and the law, and Australian values
- Australian values only: 24 must-pass values questions
- Random mode with no repeats until you have seen every question
- Key questions that people often get wrong
- Timer per question (30s to 135s real exam pace) or no timer
- Shuffle answers, with large buttons that never move

KEEP YOUR PROGRESS
- Your recent scores and progress are saved on your iPhone
- Carry on where you stopped

PRIVATE AND SIMPLE
- Works offline
- No sign-up, no ads, no tracking
- No data collected

mPractice is an unofficial practice tool. It is not made by, linked to or approved by the Australian Government or the Department of Home Affairs. The questions are not the real test questions. Always check the Home Affairs website for the latest rules.
